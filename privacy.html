Privacy policy

Petal Planner · Updated 9 November 2026

Petal Planner is a page-turning planner for iPhone, iPad and the web. It does not collect, keep or share any personal information. There are no accounts, adverts, analytics or tracking.

What you write

Everything you write, draw or choose in the planner stays on your own device. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. On iPhone and iPad, the planner is saved with the app's own data, so it is included in your device's backups, such as iCloud Backup.

Backups and calendar files

When you choose Back up planner, or export a calendar file, the planner makes the file and hands it to you. Where you save or send it is up to you.

Calendar

If you tap Add to Apple Calendar, the app asks for permission to add events, then adds your open to-dos as all-day events. It can add events but cannot read your calendar. You can turn this permission off at any time in Settings.

Camera and photos

If you pick a photo for your planner cover, the photo stays inside the planner on your device. The camera is used only if you choose to take a new photo.

Links to other services

The buttons that open Google Calendar, Outlook or iCloud take you to those websites in your browser. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it saved on that device. On the web, clearing this website's data in your browser does the same. Make a backup first if you want to keep your planner.

Children

Petal Planner does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
